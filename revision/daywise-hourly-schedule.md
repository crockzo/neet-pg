# NEET PG — Day-wise Hour-by-Hour Schedule (13–29 Aug 2026)

This file gives each of the 17 days as an hour-by-hour plan, following the **Daily hour-by-hour template** from `revision-schedule.md` (§3). Block times are fixed; chapters may spill a few minutes into the next block — anything left over goes into **Block H (23:00–00:30)**, the built-in catch-up window. PYQs always sit in the low-energy slots (C, E, H).

- **Days 1–14 (13–26 Aug) = Pass 1** for ALL 20 subjects — full PYQs (1.5 min/q). **Bookmark ~50% of each chapter's questions** as you go (those become your Pass-2 question set).
- **Days 15–17 (27–29 Aug) = Pass 2** for the 11 highest-priority subjects — notes + **only bookmarked PYQs (1 min/q)**.
- Backlog & downgrade rules: `revision-schedule.md` §7.
